# BasaltKV 1.2 datasheet

Vendor: Basalt Systems. Saved 2025-06-02.

## Overview
BasaltKV is a B-tree storage engine with a commercial licence.

## Specifications
- Replication: quorum, minimum cluster of 3 nodes.
- Vendor-tested ingest: up to 20,000 writes per second per cluster.
- Read latency: p99 typically under 4 ms.
- Supported architectures: x86-64 and ARM.

## Licensing
List price: 120 USD per node per month. Volume and annual-commitment discounts are
available from sales.
