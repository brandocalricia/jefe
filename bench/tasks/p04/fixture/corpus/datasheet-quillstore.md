# QuillStore 1.0 datasheet

Vendor: Quill Analytics. Saved 2025-07-11.

## Overview
QuillStore is a columnar append-only log engine, optimised for scans and aggregation.

## Specifications
- Replication: chain, minimum cluster of 2 nodes.
- Vendor-tested ingest: up to 60,000 writes per second per cluster.
- Read latency: p99 under 10 ms for point reads; scans are much faster than point reads.
- Supported architectures: x86-64 and ARM.

## Licensing
Per-node subscription, quoted on the pricing sheet.
